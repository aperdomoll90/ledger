Use full words, not acronyms, in document names, section headings, and prose content.

**Why:** Adrian has stated "I hate acronims" (sic) and asked to replace "IA" with "information architecture" in a filename. This is a direct preference, not a one-off correction. Applies in conversation responses too, not just files.

**How to apply:**

- Filenames: `starbrite-information-architecture-proposal.md`, not `starbrite-ia-proposal.md`.
- Section headings in docs: "Information Architecture", not "IA".
- Prose: "product detail page", not "PDP". "user experience", not "UX". "standard operating procedure", not "SOP".
- Exception: universal tech acronyms that are more familiar in acronym form: URL, HTTP, HTTPS, API, JSON, CSV, XML, PDF, SQL, HTML, CSS, JS, TS. These stay.
- Domain-specific or project-specific acronyms: spell out. If a term is less well-known, even abbreviations like IA are opaque and create friction.

**Ties to existing rule:** `feedback_explain_acronyms.md` says always explain acronyms on first use. This rule is stricter: for project-domain acronyms, prefer the full term over the acronym entirely rather than introducing the acronym + explanation. The two rules are compatible: when a universal tech acronym appears (URL, API), explain if needed; when a domain acronym is considered (IA, PDP), spell it out instead.
