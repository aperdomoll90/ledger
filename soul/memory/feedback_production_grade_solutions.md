Always suggest industry-standard, production-grade solutions when presenting options.

**Why:** Adrian builds professional tools and expects recommendations that match real-world best practices. Lightweight or minimal approaches should only appear as stepping stones toward the production-grade solution, not as standalone recommendations.

**How to apply:** When proposing approaches, lead with what a senior engineer at a well-run company would choose. Frame simpler alternatives as incremental steps toward the production solution, not as alternatives to it. Never recommend a toy or "good enough" solution when a battle-tested pattern exists.
