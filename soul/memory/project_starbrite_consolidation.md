Starbrite currently runs two web properties: `www.starbrite.com` (Shopify commerce) and `instincts.starbrite.com` (separate subdomain with editorial, brand, and About content). The current Shopify Main Menu still links off to `instincts.starbrite.com` for "Instincts Series" and "About Us".

The active restructure project **consolidates both properties onto the Shopify store.** `instincts.starbrite.com` is being absorbed, not preserved.

**Why:** Confirmed by Adrian on 2026-04-21 during the initial catalog audit. Fragmentation across domains is a known problem; the project's scope is to eliminate it.

**How to apply:**
- When designing or advising on the Starbrite design system, assume scope includes **editorial layouts** (articles, brand pages, event coverage, destination features, team profiles, deep-dive pages), not only commerce surfaces.
- The existing content-template metaobjects on the Shopify store (Standard Academy, Event Recap, Destination/Location Feature, Product Deep-Dive, The Star Brite Crew, Dyn Img Banner) are likely migration targets for content currently on `instincts.starbrite.com`.
- Information architecture must plan a single unified tree covering products, collections, editorial content, brand/about, team, events, and destinations. The current 5-item main nav is nowhere near enough.
- Do not flag "brand fragmentation" as a risk in future audits; flag it as current state that the project is resolving.

**Related context (confirmed 2026-04-21).** Prior contractors or internal attempts at a Starbrite site restructure never completed. The Shopify store carries abandoned artifacts: a placeholder `New Menu` (six items, all `#` URLs), a duplicate `Main Menu 2.0 (THM)`, and two empty `Catalog` menus. **Adrian's team is the cleanup crew, not an inheritor.** Do not treat these abandoned menus as constraints or prior-art to reconcile; they're to be deleted as part of our rebuild.
