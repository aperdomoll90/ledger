Do not edit, create, rewrite, or delete any doc under the user's project folders without Adrian's explicit approval for that specific change. This applies even when a factual correction is obvious from data we just pulled together, and even when I have already proposed the change. Proposing a change and describing what I would do does not count as approval. Wait for "yes", "do it", "proceed", "update it", or an equivalent direct greenlight before touching the file.

**Why:** Reference and working docs are carefully-maintained artifacts. Adrian wants to see and decide on changes before they land. Silent edits, even well-intended corrections, erode trust in the docs and make it hard to review what's new versus what was already agreed.

**How to apply:** When I spot something wrong in a doc, surface the finding with the specific line or section, describe what I would change and why, and stop. Do not combine the analysis with the edit in the same turn. If Adrian says yes, then edit. If he is silent or asks clarifying questions, hold. This overrides any "proactive helpfulness" default. The rule applies to interface files, architecture docs, findings and recommendations, site organization, dashboards, and every other doc under `~/Documents/`, `~/repos/`, and `~/.ledger/` unless he has preauthorized a specific workflow (like session checkpoints on a dashboard, which is already its own standing instruction).
